Everything you need to see the moon

No account, no ads, no network. All calculations run on your phone.

Live camera view

The moon, the horizon, and compass points show on top of the camera image.

Turn-by-turn pointer

When the moon is off screen, an arrow and the heading strip tell you where to turn.

Time slider

Move from 12 hours back to 48 hours ahead, or pick any date. The view updates at once.

Altitude chart

See the moon height over time on day, twilight, and night colors.

Rise, set, best view

Know the next moonrise, moonset, and the first dark-sky time. Tap to jump there.

Real phase

A realistic moon image with the correct phase, percent lit, and distance.

How it works

Three steps. It takes a few seconds.

  1. Allow camera and locationYour location stays on the phone. It is used only to calculate the moon position.
  2. Point at the skyFollow the arrow until the moon is in the target ring. The ring turns gold when you are on target.
  3. Plan your viewDrag the slider or the chart to see when the moon is high and the sky is dark.
